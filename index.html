Philosophy

We work as a circle. Every company in the group sends clients to the others, and every company answers to the same standard. A buyer who comes in through Goyal & DeCoster can finance through Red Rock Lending, partner through Red Rock Capital, and close with Red Rock Title & Legal once it opens. The client deals with one group that knows the whole file.

Principles

Creativity

We look for the structure that makes the deal work: phases, partnerships, seller financing or a straight sale.

Luxury

The experience should match the asset, and every client gets the same care at every price point.

Integrity

Terms in writing, conflicts disclosed, and every transaction done in the open.

We honor people
